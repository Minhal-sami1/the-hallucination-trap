# The Hallucination Trap

## Register

product

## Users

The main viewer is Antoine Kanaan, CEO and Co-Founder of HAQQ. Other viewers are legal-AI founders, product leaders, lawyers, and risk owners in the UAE and the wider MENA region. They already know that fluent legal text can be wrong. They need direct proof that a citation is real before legal work reaches a client.

## Product Purpose

The product is a public, independent demonstration of citation control. A user asks one UAE legal question. An ungrounded model and a grounded pipeline answer at the same time. A live audit checks each citation against an official legal corpus. The main success moment is a fabricated article marked in red within two seconds, with a precise reason and an official source link.

The product is not a chatbot and is not a report generator. It is a courtroom for citations.

## Brand Personality

Austere. Forensic. Defensible.

The voice is direct and technical. It states what the corpus can prove. It states scope limits. It does not use generic AI claims. It uses English and Arabic as equal interface languages.

## Anti-references

- No generic AI dashboard with purple gradients, glowing cards, or robot imagery.
- No marketing hero that delays the audit.
- No hidden cache state or simulated output presented as live output.
- No affiliation claim, HAQQ logo, invented testimonial, or invented metric.
- No red verdict for a citation outside the loaded corpus.
- No English-first layout with broken or partial RTL support.

## Design Principles

1. Put the citation audit in the main line of sight.
2. Show proof, not confidence language.
3. Make a refusal look like a correct control decision.
4. Keep the loaded corpus and its limits visible.
5. Give every number a repository command that reproduces it.
6. Use motion only to mark a new verdict or a changed state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard use, visible focus, screen-reader status messages, reduced motion, and non-color verdict labels. Use real RTL layout in Arabic mode. Use Arabic-capable fonts and localized numerals. Support 320 px, 375 px, 414 px, 768 px, and desktop widths without horizontal page scroll.

## Source Context

HAQQ's official site describes legal research with sources, citation audit, audit trails, jurisdiction grounding, and Arabic and UAE coverage. This independent demo targets the same trust boundary without copying HAQQ branding or claiming affiliation.
